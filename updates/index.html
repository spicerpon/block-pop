Block Pop update notes

Everything that changed in the game, newest first · Play Block Pop · Strategy guide

Block Pop is the newest of our browser games. This page is where every change lands as it ships — features, balance, fixes — so you never have to guess whether the game you remember is the game that is live.

Guided tutorial and optional ads

OCTOBER 27, 2026

  • First play now walks you through three small boards: fill a row, fill a row and a column with one piece, then chain two clears into a combo. The tutorial reserves its own band above the board so nothing jumps when it appears.
  • Rewarded continue: when no piece fits, watch an ad to sweep the board and keep your score, up to twice per run. If ads are unavailable, one continue is free.
  • Adventure levels that beat you several times in a row offer a skip after an ad.
  • A short full-screen ad may appear before a result card, and only after at least 25 seconds of play. Everything works with ads blocked.

Block Pop 1.0

OCTOBER 27, 2026 · FIRST RELEASE

  • Classic endless mode on an 8×8 board with three-piece trays, a hold box, unlimited skips and combo scoring.
  • Adventure mode: 60 preset levels with red and blue gems to collect, generated the same way for every player.
  • Fifteen piece shapes whose odds shift towards bigger pieces as your score rises.
  • Colour themes, two achievement tracks (best score and blocks popped) with bronze, silver and gold tiers, and a world ranking for Classic best scores.
  • Korean, English, Japanese, Russian, Spanish and French, detected automatically and switchable in the menu.
  • Icon and share image rendered with the game's own block art.
